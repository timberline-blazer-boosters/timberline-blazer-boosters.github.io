The Timberline High School Booster Club supports clubs, athletics, programs, and the arts at Timberline High School. The grant program is intended to support student athletics, activities, clubs, and teacher and classroom needs that benefit the general student body.

The program is funded through concessions, Kroger, and donations. To keep it viable, we ask that team or program members and families participate in Boosters fundraising. Grant requests must meet Timberline High School’s educational goals and Booster Club objectives, and must be on behalf of a recognized school program, activity, or club.

Grants will not be approved for non-school-related programs and events, or for salaries.
