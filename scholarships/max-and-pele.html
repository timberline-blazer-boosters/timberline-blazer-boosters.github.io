Pele and Max exemplified what it meant to be a Blazer athlete. They worked hard, excelled on the field and in the classroom, and consistently uplifted those around them. Both were regular supporters of all Timberline events. This scholarship honors their memories and encourages others to excel in the ways they did.

The Pele Maiava Max Aunese Memorial Scholarship awards $500 to selected Timberline High School graduating seniors who exemplify the character and lived experience of those young men.

If you have questions about this scholarship, please contact the Booster Club.
