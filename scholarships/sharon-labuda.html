Sharon LaBuda (June 30, 1951 – December 26, 2023) was an integral part of the Timberline High School staff. She joined the school in 1987 and served faithfully until her passing — more than 36 years as a counselor, colleague, and friend to thousands of students in the Lacey community.

In a world where you can be anything — be kind.

If you have questions about the Sharon LaBuda Memorial Scholarship or the application process, please contact the Booster Club.
