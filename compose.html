Compose a post

Officers: crop one photo, pick several for a swipe post, or drop a finished MP4 reel (with audio already in the file). Photos become a news post. A reel goes to Facebook and Instagram only — it is not added to the website. Instagram uses the Facebook Page login in Make, not a developer app.

Media type
Image (required)

Drop a photo here or click to choose. Drag to position it in the 4:5 frame.

Choose 2–10 photos. The page squares each one automatically. You do not crop them one by one.

Drop a finished 9:16 MP4 (keep it under about 80 MB). Audio should already be in the file. This does not create a news post. Make hosts the file only long enough for Instagram to fetch it, then deletes it from the site.

What Make receives

One photo: crop it in the 4:5 frame. Make commits it; Facebook and Instagram follow from the news feed. Multiple photos: pick 2–10 pictures. The site shows them; Instagram gets a swipe post; Facebook gets the blog link. MP4 reel: Facebook and Instagram only — no news post. Check Website only on photo posts to skip Instagram and Facebook.

If a post with that title already exists for today, change the title and try again. Do not overwrite posts.
