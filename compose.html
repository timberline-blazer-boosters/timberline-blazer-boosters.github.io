Compose a post

Officers: this publishes a news post on the site and in the RSS feed for Instagram. Pages may take about a minute to rebuild. Use a different password than the list-upload form if you set one in Make.

Photo for Instagram

Make converts the upload to a 1080×1350 JPEG (4:5). JPEG is preferred. Avoid very tall portraits (2:3); Make will crop from the center. The same photo appears in the post and in the feed Instagram reads.

If a post with that title already exists for today, change the title and try again. Do not overwrite posts.
