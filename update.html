Update lists

Officers: upload a CSV to replace the membership roster or the donor ticker. Each upload replaces the whole list. Pages may take about a minute to rebuild.

CSV format

Excel: File → Save As → CSV UTF-8. You may include full last names; Make stores only the first letter on the site.

Donors

first_name,last_name,amount
John,Doe,25.00
Jane,Smith,100

Visitors see John D — $25.00 on the Donate page ticker.

Members

first_name,last_name,membership_type
Jane,Doe,Family (Annual)
Alex,Rivera,Individual

Visitors see Jane D and the membership type on the Membership page.
