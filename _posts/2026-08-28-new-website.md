The Timberline Blazers Booster Club has a new home on the web. This site is where you can follow club news, find upcoming events, apply for scholarships and grants, donate, and get in touch.

Use the menu to visit **Events** for the calendar, **Scholarships & Grants** for applications, **Donate** to support our students, **Gallery** for photos, and **Contact** if you want to volunteer or have a question.

We are glad you are here. Go Blazers!
